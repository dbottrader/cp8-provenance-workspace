On **August 15, 2002**, one year and four days after the Chilbolton "Arecibo Reply," a formation of unprecedented artistic and technical complexity appeared at **Crabwood Farm**, near the village of Sparsholt, approximately 8 miles from the Chilbolton site in Hampshire, UK. This formation departed radically from the geometric abstraction of most crop circles. It depicted something unmistakable: a **face**.

Not just any face. A **grey alien face** — large dark eyes, narrow chin, small mouth, triangular head — rendered in a **halftone raster technique** that allowed shading and depth through varying densities of flattened crop cells. Beside the face, a separate circular formation: a **disc** filled with a spiral of tiny blocks.

The disc was not decorative. When decoded by British computer consultant and crop circle researcher **Paul Vigay**, the spiral of blocks resolved into **8-bit binary ASCII code**. The message, read from the center outward in a counterclockwise spiral, was in English.

What it said changed everything.

## High-Resolution Pattern Description

### Part 1: The Face (Rectangular Raster)

The left portion of the formation is a **rectangle** containing a pixel-shaded portrait. The technique is halftone rasterization — the same method used in newspaper printing and early digital images:

- **Dense clusters** of flattened wheat = dark areas (eyes, shadows)
- **Sparse or upright** wheat = light areas (skin, highlights)
- **Mid-density** clusters = intermediate tones (contours, depth)

The result is a **photorealistic alien face** viewed in three-quarter profile. Large black eyes. Narrow nasal bridge. Small mouth. High cranial dome. The classic "grey" morphology that had permeated popular culture through decades of UFO reports, abduction accounts, and science fiction.

What distinguishes this from any human-made art is the **scale and precision**. At ground level, the image is invisible — just a field of flattened and upright wheat. From 500 feet in the air, the face emerges with eerie clarity. The shading gradients are smooth. The proportions are anatomically consistent. The eyes stare directly at the viewer with an expression that is neither threatening nor welcoming — simply **present**.

### Part 2: The Disc (Circular Binary Spiral)

To the right of the face is a **perfect circle** — the "disc" the alien appears to hold. Inside this circle, a **single continuous spiral** of tiny rectangular blocks winds from the center outward in a counterclockwise direction.

Each block is a binary digit:
- **Flattened block** = binary 1
- **Upright / absent block** = binary 0
- **Small tuft markers** = byte separators (8-bit grouping)

The spiral contains **1,368 bits**, organized as **171 eight-bit bytes**. Read as standard ASCII characters, the bytes spell out a message.

### The Decoded Message

> **"Beware the bearers of FALSE gifts & their BROKEN PROMISES. Much PAIN but still time. BELIEVE. There is GOOD out there. We OPpose DECEPTION. Conduit CLOSING"**

*(Some decodings include a final "(BELL SOUND)" or "\a" — the ASCII bell character, 0x07)*

## Binary Disc Decode: ASCII / Hex Analysis

### The Decoding Method (Paul Vigay, 2002)

1. **Photograph the disc** from directly above at high resolution
2. **Trace the spiral** from center outward, counterclockwise
3. **Identify each block**: flattened = 1, upright/gap = 0
4. **Group into bytes**: 8 bits per byte, separated by marker tufts
5. **Map to ASCII**: each byte = one ASCII character
6. **Read the resulting text**

Beware the bearers of FALSE gifts & their BROKEN PROMISES.
